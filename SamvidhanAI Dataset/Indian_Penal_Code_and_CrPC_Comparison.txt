DOCUMENT: Indian Penal Code 1860, CrPC 1973 & Indian Evidence Act 1872 (Cross-Comparison to BNS, BNSS & BSA 2023)
ACT NAME: IPC 1860, CrPC 1973 and IEA 1872 Comparative Legal Reference
CATEGORY: criminal_and_evidence_law
JURISDICTION: India
YEAR: 1860 / 1872 / 1973 / 2023

SECTION MAPPING: INDIAN PENAL CODE (IPC) TO BHARATIYA NYAYA SANHITA (BNS 2023)
1. Section 302 IPC (Punishment for Murder) → Maps to Section 103 BNS 2023.
   Punishment: Death or imprisonment for life, and fine. Section 103(2) BNS adds distinct death/life imprisonment for mob lynching by 5 or more persons.
2. Section 300 IPC (Definition of Murder) → Maps to Section 101 BNS 2023.
3. Section 299 IPC (Culpable Homicide) → Maps to Section 100 BNS 2023.
4. Section 304 IPC (Punishment for Culpable Homicide not amounting to murder) → Maps to Section 105 BNS 2023.
5. Section 304A IPC (Causing death by rash/negligent act) → Maps to Section 106 BNS 2023 (Includes 106(2) aggravated 10-year penalty for hit-and-run without reporting).
6. Section 304B IPC (Dowry Death) → Maps to Section 80 BNS 2023 (Punishment: Imprisonment not less than 7 years extending to life).
7. Section 375 & 376 IPC (Rape & Punishment) → Maps to Section 63 & 64 BNS 2023 (Minimum 10 years extending to life).
8. Section 377 IPC (Unnatural Offences) → Omitted in BNS 2023 (Consensual acts between adults decriminalized per Navtej Singh Johar).
9. Section 498A IPC (Cruelty by Husband or Relatives) → Maps to Section 85 & 86 BNS 2023 (Up to 3 years imprisonment and fine).
10. Section 354 IPC (Outraging modesty of woman) → Maps to Section 74 BNS 2023.
11. Section 354C IPC (Voyeurism) → Maps to Section 77 BNS 2023.
12. Section 354D IPC (Stalking) → Maps to Section 78 BNS 2023.
13. Section 378 & 379 IPC (Theft & Punishment) → Maps to Section 303 BNS 2023 (Includes community service for theft < Rs 5000 first offence).
14. Section 420 IPC (Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery) → Maps to Section 318(4) BNS 2023 (Imprisonment up to 7 years and fine).
15. Section 405 & 406 IPC (Criminal Breach of Trust) → Maps to Section 316 BNS 2023.
16. Section 499 & 500 IPC (Defamation & Punishment) → Maps to Section 356 BNS 2023 (Simple imprisonment up to 2 years, fine, or community service).
17. Section 506 IPC (Criminal Intimidation) → Maps to Section 351 BNS 2023.
18. Section 124A IPC (Sedition) → Repealed and replaced by Section 152 BNS 2023 (Acts endangering sovereignty, unity and integrity of India).
19. New BNS Offences without direct IPC counterpart:
    - Section 69 BNS: Sexual intercourse by deceitful means / false promise of marriage (up to 10 years).
    - Section 109 BNS: Organized Crime (Death/Life and minimum Rs 10 Lakh fine).
    - Section 111 BNS: Terrorist Acts incorporated into general criminal code.
    - Section 112 BNS: Petty Organized Crime (1 to 7 years).
    - Section 4(f) BNS: Community Service as a statutory sentence.

SECTION MAPPING: CODE OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE (CrPC) TO BNSS 2023
1. Section 154 CrPC (FIR) → Maps to Section 173 BNSS 2023 (Zero FIR, electronic FIR e-FIR within 3 days, preliminary enquiry for 3-7 year offences).
2. Section 41 CrPC (Arrest without warrant & Arnesh Kumar safeguards) → Maps to Section 35 BNSS 2023.
3. Section 41A CrPC (Notice of Appearance) → Maps to Section 35(3) BNSS 2023.
4. Section 57 CrPC (24-hour limit on police custody) → Maps to Section 58 BNSS 2023.
5. Section 161 CrPC (Police examination of witnesses) → Maps to Section 180 BNSS 2023 (Audio-video electronic recording allowed).
6. Section 164 CrPC (Statements & Confessions before Magistrate) → Maps to Section 183 BNSS 2023.
7. Section 167 CrPC (Remand and Default Bail) → Maps to Section 187 BNSS 2023 (Police custody 15 days in whole or parts across initial 40/60 days; default bail after 60/90 days).
8. Section 176 CrPC (Inquiry into custodial death) → Maps to Section 176 BNSS 2023 (Mandatory Judicial Magistrate inquiry, post-mortem videography, NHRC notification within 24 hours).
9. Section 436A CrPC (Maximum undertrial detention) → Maps to Section 479 BNSS 2023 (Release on bail after one-half time served; ONE-THIRD time for first-time offenders).
10. Section 437 CrPC (Bail in non-bailable offences) → Maps to Section 480 BNSS 2023.
11. Section 438 CrPC (Anticipatory Bail) → Maps to Section 482 BNSS 2023.
12. Section 439 CrPC (Special powers of Sessions Court / High Court for bail) → Maps to Section 483 BNSS 2023.
13. Section 482 CrPC (Inherent powers of High Court / Quashing) → Maps to Section 528 BNSS 2023.

SECTION MAPPING: INDIAN EVIDENCE ACT (IEA 1872) TO BHARATIYA SAKSHYA ADHINIYAM (BSA 2023)
1. Section 108 IEA (Presumption of Civil Death after 7 Years) → Maps to Section 111 BSA 2023.
   Rule: A person is legally presumed dead (Civil Death) if not heard of for 7 (seven) years by those who would naturally have heard of them.
2. Section 107 IEA (Presumption of Life within 30 Years) → Maps to Section 110 BSA 2023.
3. Section 32(1) IEA (Dying Declaration) → Maps to Section 26(a) BSA 2023.
4. Section 25 IEA (Confession to police inadmissible) → Maps to Section 22 BSA 2023.
5. Section 26 IEA (Confession in police custody inadmissible unless before Magistrate) → Maps to Section 23 BSA 2023.
6. Section 27 IEA (Information leading to discovery of fact) → Maps to Section 24 BSA 2023.
7. Section 65B IEA (Admissibility of Electronic Evidence Certificate) → Maps to Section 57 & Section 63 BSA 2023.
8. Section 101 IEA (Burden of Proof) → Maps to Section 104 BSA 2023.
9. Section 105 IEA (Burden of proving general exceptions) → Maps to Section 108 BSA 2023.
10. Section 113A IEA (Presumption as to abetment of suicide of married woman) → Maps to Section 116 BSA 2023.
11. Section 113B IEA (Presumption as to Dowry Death) → Maps to Section 117 BSA 2023.
12. Section 115 IEA (Estoppel) → Maps to Section 121 BSA 2023.
13. Section 133 IEA (Accomplice testimony) → Maps to Section 138 BSA 2023.
14. Section 154 IEA (Hostile Witness) → Maps to Section 145 BSA 2023.
